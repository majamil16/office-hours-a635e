How this works (for you)

  1. Asker submits on the site. We record the request and hold the amount (see payments below).
  2. You DM the host on Jelly as @officehours — copy the message below, send it, then click Mark host notified.
  3. Host accepts or declines. Accept in ops when they agree; Decline refunds the asker (in our system) and gives you asker DM text.
  4. Host delivers on Jelly. When the answer jelly is posted, click Mark delivered to capture payment.
